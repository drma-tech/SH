# Roadmap

This roadmap describes areas being explored for future development. It is not a delivery commitment, and priorities may change.

Shared Home focuses on discovering rooms in already-occupied shared properties and helping prospective residents discover the people they may live with. It is a discovery and advertising platform, not a rental agency or payment intermediary. Rental agreements, negotiations, and rent payments remain between the people involved.

## Phase 1: Marketplace Foundation

- Guest Room-Wanted Ads
- Public Discovery of Room Listings and Seeker Profiles
- Resident Profiles Linked to Shared Properties
- Improved Room, Property, and Profile Search Filters
- Saved Rooms and Profiles
- Direct Communication Between Prospective Residents and Hosts

## Phase 2: Trust and Compatibility

- Modular Identity Verification
- Country-Specific Verification Options
- Background Check Integrations
- Financial and Income Verification Options
- Structured Lifestyle and Shared-Living Preferences
- Roommate Compatibility Indicators

## Phase 3: Marketplace Growth and Promotion

- Promoted Room Listings
- Promoted Seeker Profiles
- Featured Host and Resident Profiles
- Paid Proposal Visibility and Priority Options
- Competitive Proposal Features
- Marketplace Discovery and Engagement Improvements

## Phase 4: Shared Living Community

- Private Communities for Existing Residents
- Resident-to-Resident Messaging
- Host Announcements and Notice Board
- Shared Property Updates and Community Information
- Resident Interaction Tools

## Future Development

- Personalized Room and People Recommendations
- Advanced Compatibility Matching
- Market-Specific Features and Integrations
- Additional Tools for Shared Property Communities
